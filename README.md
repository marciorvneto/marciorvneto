### Hi, I'm Márcio

Chemical engineer from Belo Horizonte, Brazil. I did my PhD on Kraft evaporator plants
(UFMG and LUT University in Finland), and really enjoy writing scientific software:
simulators, solvers, the numerical bits underneath.

These days I do consulting at Bloom Consultoria, though it's rarely typical engineering:
the nastiest problems tend to land on my desk, and we usually end up building tooling to figure out what to do.
Some of those tools turn into research and, every once in a while, a paper. I also work on [Voima](https://voimatoolbox.com),
a calculation platform that over 4,500 engineers use, and lately I've been playing with physics-informed neural networks for process plants,
which is where a lot of my side projects are heading.

I like my tools small, readable, and with as few dependencies as possible.

**Right now**

I'm writing [**Aktua**](https://github.com/marciorvneto/aktua), a little programming
language for control systems, in C. Transfer functions, matrices, ODEs and plots, all
in about 150 KB. It's my excuse to learn how compilers work and get a little better at classical control,
and it's turning into something I actually use ([aktua.dev](https://aktua.dev)).
